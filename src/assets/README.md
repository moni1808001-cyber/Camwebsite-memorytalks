# Memory Talks — Assets & Media Guide

Welcome to your automated photo, video, and commercial showcase management system!

## 📁 Folder Structure

Place your media files directly into the corresponding category folders below:

```text
src/
└── assets/
    ├── images/
    │   ├── wedding/         <- Place Wedding photos here
    │   ├── pre-wedding/     <- Place Pre-Wedding photos here
    │   ├── post-wedding/    <- Place Post-Wedding photos here
    │   ├── baby-shower/     <- Place Baby Shower photos here
    │   ├── vehicle-bike/    <- Place Vehicle Bike photos here (e.g. bike1.jpeg, bike2.jpeg, bike3.jpeg)
    │   ├── vehicle-car/     <- Place Vehicle Car photos here (e.g. car1.jpeg)
    │   ├── influencer/      <- Place Influencer photos here (e.g. influ1.jpeg, influ2.jpeg, influ3.jpeg)
    │   └── about-us/        <- Place About Us photos here (aboutus1, aboutus2)
    └── videos/
        ├── wedding/         <- Place Wedding videos here
        ├── pre-wedding/     <- Place Pre-Wedding videos here
        ├── post-wedding/    <- Place Post-Wedding videos here
        └── baby-shower/     <- Place Baby Shower videos here
```

---

## 📷 Supported Formats

### Photos
* `.jpg` / `.jpeg`
* `.png`
* `.webp`

### Videos
* `.mp4`
* `.webm`

---

## ⚡ Photo Categories & Galleries

All 7 categories are available under the **Photo** menu in the header and in the main **Photos Gallery**:
1. **Wedding** (`src/assets/images/wedding/`)
2. **Pre-Wedding** (`src/assets/images/pre-wedding/`)
3. **Post-Wedding** (`src/assets/images/post-wedding/`)
4. **Baby Shower** (`src/assets/images/baby-shower/`)
5. **Vehicle Bike** (`src/assets/images/vehicle-bike/`)
6. **Vehicle Car** (`src/assets/images/vehicle-car/`)
7. **Influencer** (`src/assets/images/influencer/`)

Each category has its own dedicated folder. Whenever you add photos to these folders, they automatically appear in the respective gallery with lightbox zoom and next/previous navigation!
